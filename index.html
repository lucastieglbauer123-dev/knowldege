02 / technical profile

How I work.

Useful beats impressive.

Most of my work comes from running servers and building the tools I wish I had. That means I care about readable logs, predictable configuration, performance that stays steady, and systems that are still understandable a month later.

I’m strongest where Minecraft development meets real operations—where code has to work for players, staff, and the person who has to debug it at two in the morning.

01
Build for the actual use caseStart with the problem players or staff are really having, then keep the solution focused.
02
Read the evidenceLogs, profiles, and reproducible tests beat guessing when mods and plugins interact.
03
Leave things clearerGood systems should be easier to operate after they are built, not harder.
